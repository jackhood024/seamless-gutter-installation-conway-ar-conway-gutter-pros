Overflowing gutters in Conway: a symptom-by-symptom guide

Symptom: water pours off at the downspout opening

A clogged downspout is a pipe packed with leaves, pine needles and grit, so the trough above it backs up and spills. A gutter guide read for this page lists "Leaves, pine needles, shingle grit, and decomposed organic material" as what blocks the channel or the downspout opening. Pine needles and sweetgum balls are everyday Conway yard debris.

Checking a clogged downspout from the ground

Hold a garden hose at the top of the downspout and watch the bottom. Weak flow or none means a plug. Conway Gutter Pros clears a downspout the same way, with a hose first, then a plumber's snake or the elbow taken off.

When a downspout clog needs a crew

Call about a plug that a hose will not move. The crew clears downspouts above ground only, and buried drain lines are not part of the job.

Symptom: water spills along the middle of the run

Mid-run overflow is a trough so full of wet leaves that the water has nowhere to go but over the edge. The gutter looks fine from the driveway, then fails in the first hard rain.

Reading a mid-run spill during a storm

Stand in the yard during a storm and note exactly where the spill starts. Look at the roofline for tufts of grass or small seedlings, which mean old debris is sitting in the trough. Oaks around Thousand Oaks and pines out toward Preston drop plenty of both.

When a packed trough needs a crew

Call if you would need a ladder to look closer. Many customers are homeowners who no longer want to climb one, and Conway Gutter Pros cleans one-story and two-story homes. Nobody has to be home, since access to the gate or yard is all the crew needs.

Symptom: it still overflows after a cleaning

Overflow from a clean gutter is a sign the problem is the gutter itself, not what is inside it. Two causes are worth knowing. The first is slope. The same gutter guide describes a proper pitch as "1/4 inch per 10 feet toward the downspout." The second is size, and the guide puts it this way: "Your gutters are too small for your roof's water volume."

Checking pitch and size after a cleaning

After a cleaning and a flushed downspout, watch the next storm. If water still jumps the edge, the trough may be sagging, tilted away from the outlet or undersized.

Who to phone when a clean gutter still overflows

Symptom: water pools at the foundation or in the crawlspace

Water at the foundation after a Conway storm is often the downspout and gutter overflow dumping the whole roof's runoff beside the house. People often call when gutters overflow in the rain and water gathers there or gets into the crawlspace.

Tracing foundation water to the gutter

See whether the spot sits under a spill point you noticed earlier, and whether the downspout runs at all.

When foundation water needs a cleaning

Call right away for the cleaning, since a clear gutter and downspout are the first thing to rule out. Standing water inside the crawlspace itself is a separate matter to raise with a contractor.

Do seamless gutters overflow less?

Seamless gutters have fewer joints along the run, which helps with leaking seams. They do not stop leaves, needles and sweetgum balls from filling the trough or plugging an elbow. A new seamless run still needs cleaning, and an old one that overflows may only need a clean. Guards do not end the job either, because pine needles and sweetgum balls still get into guarded gutters.
